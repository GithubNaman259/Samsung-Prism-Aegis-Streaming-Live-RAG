# Cancellation Policy — Standard Events

## Pune Venue Cancellation Policy

This cancellation policy applies specifically to the following approved Pune venues:
- Orchid Hall
- Sahyadri Conference Centre
- Riverside Studio
- Baner Annexe

### Pune Cancellation Notice Tiers

For Orchid Hall, Sahyadri Conference Centre, Riverside Studio, and Baner Annexe in Pune, cancellations made more than 14 days before the event date incur no charge.

For Orchid Hall, Sahyadri Conference Centre, Riverside Studio, and Baner Annexe in Pune, cancellations between 7 and 14 days before the event incur 25 percent of the venue fee.

For Orchid Hall, Sahyadri Conference Centre, Riverside Studio, and Baner Annexe in Pune, cancellations inside 7 days incur 50 percent of the venue fee.

For Orchid Hall, Sahyadri Conference Centre, Riverside Studio, and Baner Annexe in Pune, cancellations inside 48 hours incur the full venue fee.

### Pune Cancellation Authorization

For Pune venues, a cancellation inside the 7 day window must be authorised by the event owner's reporting manager. A cancellation inside 48 hours must be authorised by a senior director, because the full fee is charged to the department cost centre.

### Pune Rescheduling

For Pune venues, moving an event to a new date more than 14 days away is treated as a reschedule, not a cancellation, and carries no fee. Only one free reschedule is permitted per booking; a second date change is charged at the applicable cancellation tier.
